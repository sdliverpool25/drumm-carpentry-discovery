# Drumm Carpentry discovery

An editable, public GitHub Pages workspace capturing Digital Drumm’s discovery session with Mary (bookkeeper) at Drumm Carpentry, November 2026.

**Live page:** https://sdliverpool25.github.io/drumm-carpentry-discovery/

## What it contains

- Key notes on deposits, staged payments, the Schedule Sheet, and fixed payments
- A Miro-style whiteboard (Excalidraw) for stickies, arrows and sketches, with fullscreen, scene export/import and PNG export
- A stakeholder map: organisation chart (Darren at the top; Mary, Lami, Tony and Ines reporting; Gavin as a dotted external line) and a toggle to a power/interest grid, plus role cards for responsibilities and pain points
- Tech stack: a Mermaid architecture diagram of today’s systems and manual data flows, with a Future state toggle in which the custom ERP is the system of record
- Process maps for daily bank reconciliation, supplier invoice processing, and quote → deposit → schedule → invoice
- Use cases #1–#9, with the creditors report pinned at the top
- The September 2026 monthly reports checklist

Everything on the page is editable. Changes autosave in the current browser only (`localStorage`). They are not written back to GitHub. Use **Export JSON** or **Download HTML** to keep a copy, and **Import JSON** to load it on another device. The whiteboard also has its own `.excalidraw` scene export.

## Privacy

This site is public. Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, IBAN/BIC/sort codes, account numbers, VAT/CRO numbers and exact loan amounts are not included. Quote figures are generic examples only.
